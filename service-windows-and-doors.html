The install

Why installation beats specifications.

Even a top-rated window will leak air and water if the rough opening isn't prepared correctly. We flash, foam and trim every opening to manufacturer specification — because that's where the performance actually comes from.

  • Rough opening inspected and prepared
  • Shimmed and leveled before fastening
  • Flashing membrane at sill before window sets
  • Low-expansion foam at jambs
  • Interior and exterior trim caulked and painted

Questions

Straight answers on windows and doors.

Q01How long does window replacement take?
Most single-window replacements are done in under two hours. Full-house window jobs (10–20 windows) typically run two to three days. Doors are usually a half-day to full day per opening. We schedule one crew per job so it gets done cleanly and without interruption.
Q02How much do replacement windows cost in Staunton, VA?
Window costs vary by size, style and frame material. Budget $400–$900 per window installed for standard vinyl double-hung windows; more for specialty shapes, larger openings, or fiberglass frames. Full-house replacements in the Valley typically run $8,000–$20,000. Free estimate includes measurements and a written quote.
Q03What's the energy savings from new windows?
Replacing single-pane or low-quality double-pane windows with Energy Star certified low-E units can reduce heat loss by 25–50% through the glass alone. Combined with proper sealing at the frame, most homeowners notice a meaningful reduction in heating and cooling costs in the first winter.
Q04Full-frame or insert replacement — which do you recommend?
Insert replacements work when the existing frame and sill are solid — it's faster and less disruptive. Full-frame replacements are necessary when the frame is rotted, out of square, or you're changing the rough opening size. We make this call at the estimate after inspecting the opening.
Q05What door brands do you install?
We work with Therma-Tru, ProVia, and Pella for fiberglass and steel entry doors, and carry matching storm door lines. If you have a preference, bring it up at the estimate and we'll source accordingly.
Q06Can you match the exterior trim to my existing siding?
Yes. Trim is finished to match your existing exterior profile and color before we leave. Interior casing is matched to your existing interior profile, or we can supply a new profile if you're updating the look.
